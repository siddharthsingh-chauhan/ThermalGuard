What ThermalGuard changes on this PC
====================================

ThermalGuard switches your CPU between quiet, balanced and performance modes
depending on what you are doing, and limits the CPU clock when it gets hot.
To do that, Setup and the app make these changes:

  * A power plan named "ThermalGuard" (a copy of Windows' Balanced plan) is
    created and made active while ThermalGuard runs. Only its processor
    settings are changed. Your other power plans are not modified, and the plan
    you used before is switched back on when you exit or uninstall.

  * A Task Scheduler task named "ThermalGuard" starts the app at every logon
    for your account, with administrator rights and without a UAC prompt
    (changing power settings needs them).

  * Settings and logs are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ThermalGuard.

Optional, only if you tick them on the next pages:

  * Install LibreHardwareMonitor (downloaded with winget) and start it at
    logon, so ThermalGuard can read the CPU temperature.

  * Set closing the lid on battery, and pressing the power button, to
    Hibernate in all power plans.

ThermalGuard does not send any data anywhere. It only talks to
LibreHardwareMonitor on this PC (http://127.0.0.1:8085).

Uninstall from Settings > Apps > ThermalGuard: this removes the task and the
ThermalGuard power plan and switches your previous plan back on. Lid and
power button settings and LibreHardwareMonitor are left as they are.
